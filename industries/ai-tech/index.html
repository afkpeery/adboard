AI & Tech / Campaign field notes

Big ideas deserve a bigger stage.

A strong technology campaign turns a complicated product into a clear reason to care. A skyline placement gives your idea a moment of focus; the landing page should make the next step equally clear.

Who you could reach

Founders, developers, product teams, and people looking for better ways to work.

Lead with the outcome

Use the billboard to explain one useful result: a faster workflow, a clearer decision, or a task made easier. Save the model names and feature matrix for the destination page. A short headline and a recognizable visual are easier to understand while visitors explore the city.

Match the destination to the promise

Send curious visitors to a focused product demo, a useful example, or a concise getting-started guide. Keep the billboard headline and landing-page headline aligned, and show the product in action before asking for a commitment.

Plan the launch in stages

Try a smaller placement to compare creative ideas before committing to a larger footprint. Measure qualified visits and completed trials alongside clicks. A larger sign improves prominence, but the offer and destination still determine whether attention becomes interest.

Three ways to start

  • An interactive product demo
  • A developer-tool launch
  • A focused SaaS free trial
